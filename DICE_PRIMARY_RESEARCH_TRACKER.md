## Respondent Sampling

### Creator quota

- BPC creators: makeup, skincare, haircare, grooming.
- Non-BPC creators: fashion, home, lifestyle, or general affiliate.
- Mix small, medium, and experienced creators.
- Prefer at least two vernacular or Tier 2/3 audience profiles.
- Record whether the respondent is active on Meesho Creator Club; do not assume it.

### User quota

- Recent online BPC buyer.
- Include Meesho and non-Meesho shoppers where possible.
- Capture metro and non-metro contexts.
- Ask about the last real purchase, not hypothetical preferences.

### Seller quota

- BPC seller, brand operator, creator manager, or influencer agency practitioner.
- Capture category, price band, and whether they work with small/vernacular creators.

## Interview Coding Rules

For every answer, capture:

1. Recent behavior and concrete example.
2. Funnel node affected.
3. Evidence tag: `H1`, `H2`, `H3`, `H4`, `H5`, `H0`, `N1`, or `NEW`.
4. Polarity: `Supports`, `Contradicts`, or `Neutral`.
5. Economic outcome: `NMV`, `conversion`, `return`, `repeat`, `creator retention`, `brand re-spend`, or `unclear`.
6. Confidence: `Low`, `Medium`, or `High`.
7. Verbatim quote only with permission.

## Minimum Evidence Standard

Do not promote an observation to a finding unless:

- it is based on a concrete recent behavior or direct product observation;
- the source and date are recorded;
- contradictions are logged;
- the affected funnel node is explicit;
- the commercial implication is reasoned, not assumed;
- the sample denominator is shown for primary research.
